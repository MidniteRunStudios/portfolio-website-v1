At Midnite.Run, We believe Knowledge becomes most powerful when it is free, shared, and accessible to everyone, because open access fuels creativity, equality, and collective progress. A world where information flows freely is a world where anyone can learn, grow, and contribute to something bigger than themselves.

Websites we recommend

These are websites we use regularly, and recommend to anyone looking for resources in game development, art, or software engineering.


PoseMy.ART An online pose catalogue and reference tool for artists, featuring a vast library of 3D models in various poses, customizable lighting, and camera angles to help artists create dynamic and accurate figure drawings.

KENNEY.NL A free asset pack storefront for game developers, featuring a wide range of 2D and 3D assets, including sprites, models, and sound effects.

Kay Lousberg's Game Assets A collection of free game assets, including sprites, models, and sound effects, curated/created by Kay Lousberg.

OPENGAMEART.ORG A community-driven platform for sharing free game assets, including sprites, models, textures, and sound effects, contributed by artists and developers worldwide.

INCOMPETECH.COM A website offering a wide range of royalty-free music tracks and sound effects, created by composer Kevin MacLeod, available for use in various projects, including games, videos, and other

NEWGROUNDS.COM RESOURCE WIKI A curated list of royalty-free music resources, including links to websites and platforms where creators can find free music tracks for their projects, contributed by the Newgrounds community.
