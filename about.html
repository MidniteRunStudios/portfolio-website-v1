About Midnite.Run

Starting our journey in 2009, growing out of the wild, experimental energy of the What If Zone webcomic and Flash projects that came before it.

What started as a scrappy corner of the internet has become a proud indie creative collective — building worlds, telling stories, and shipping games that wear their heart on their sleeve.

We design games, shape digital worlds, animate characters, illustrate art, engineer mods and utilities, and tell stories through prose and comics — all while passing on the knowledge that fuels our craft.

Our History

Our history is a timeline of experiments, comebacks, and game projects that shaped who we are today:

  • Long ago – What If Zone (Comic Series / Flash Animation / Web Design)
  • 2009 – Scarred (Flash Game)
  • 2010 – Runner (Adventure Game Studio)
  • 2011 – Scars of Kalakar: Emergence (RPG Maker)
  • 2013 – Scars of Kalakar (Comic Series)
  • 2014 – 2018 – Studio disbanded / hiatus
  • 2018 – Fletcher and the Mole Kingdom (Adventure Game Studio)
  • 2024 – The Best Medicine (Godot)
  • 2024 – Project Gladiator (Godot)
  • 2024 – Starship 101 (Unity)
  • 2025 – Bubble Blaster (Unity)
  • 2026 – Masquerade (Godot)
